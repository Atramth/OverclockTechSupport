# Overclock 隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective Date：2026-09-15**

---

## 中文版

### 1. 概述

Overclock（以下简称「本应用」）是一款认知训练应用。我们极度重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的数据。

核心承诺：**本应用不注册账户、不接入第三方统计/广告、不做跨应用追踪，你的所有训练数据都只保存在你自己的设备上。**

### 2. 我们收集的信息

**2.1 健康数据（睡眠）**
在你明确授权后，本应用通过 Apple HealthKit 读取你的「睡眠分析」数据，**仅在本机**用于计算睡眠质量与认知就绪度评分。这些数据**永不上传、永不共享、永不离开你的设备**。你随时可在系统「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备」中关闭授权。

**2.2 训练与进度数据**
所有训练记录、等级、积分、货币、设置、成就等，仅存储在你的设备本地（SwiftData 与 UserDefaults）。我们没有任何服务器，无法读取这些数据。

**2.3 购买数据**
应用内购买（订阅与消耗品）由 Apple 全权处理。我们无法接触你的付款信息、卡号或账单地址，仅通过 StoreKit 获取「是否已购买」状态以解锁对应功能。

### 3. 我们不收集的信息

- 不要求注册账户，不收集姓名、邮箱、手机号等身份信息。
- 不使用第三方分析 SDK，不含广告，不含任何形式的用户追踪。
- 不使用 IDFA 或任何设备标识符进行跨应用追踪。

### 4. 数据使用目的

- 根据睡眠质量计算认知就绪度，提供训练建议。
- 在征得你同意后，发送本地「作息提醒」通知（仅本地通知，不经过服务器）。

### 5. 数据共享

我们**不出售、不出租、不共享**你的任何数据给任何第三方。

### 6. 数据存储与删除

- 所有数据仅存储在你的设备本地。
- 卸载本应用即删除全部数据；也可在应用内「系统设置 → 清除缓存」删除。

### 7. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 8. 你的权利

由于所有数据都保存在你的本机、无账户体系，你始终保有对数据的完全控制：随时通过卸载或清除缓存彻底删除。

### 9. 政策更新

本政策如有重大变更，我们会在应用内或更新说明中告知。

### 10. 联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请联系：**YOUR_EMAIL**

## English

### 1. Overview

Overclock (the "App") is a cognitive-training application. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

Core commitment: **The App has no account system, no third-party analytics or ads, and no cross-app tracking. All your training data stays on your own device.**

### 2. Information We Collect

**2.1 Health Data (Sleep)**
With your explicit consent, the App reads your "Sleep Analysis" data via Apple HealthKit **solely on-device** to compute a sleep-quality and cognitive-readiness score. This data is **never uploaded, never shared, and never leaves your device**. You can revoke access anytime in System Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

**2.2 Training & Progress Data**
All training records, levels, points, currencies, settings, and achievements are stored locally on your device only (SwiftData and UserDefaults). We operate no servers and cannot read this data.

**2.3 Purchase Data**
In-app purchases (subscriptions and consumables) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, card number, or billing address. We only receive a "purchased or not" status via StoreKit to unlock the corresponding feature.

### 3. Information We Do Not Collect

- No account registration; we do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
- No third-party analytics SDKs, no ads, no user tracking of any kind.
- No use of IDFA or any device identifier for cross-app tracking.

### 4. How We Use Data

- Compute cognitive readiness from sleep quality and provide training suggestions.
- Send optional local "circadian reminder" notifications (local notifications only; no server involved).

### 5. Data Sharing

We **do not sell, rent, or share** your data with any third party.

### 6. Data Storage & Deletion

- All data is stored locally on your device.
- Uninstalling the App deletes all data; you can also delete it in the in-app "System Settings → Clear Cache".

### 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### 8. Your Rights

Because all data resides on your device with no account system, you retain full control and may permanently delete it at any time by uninstalling or clearing the cache.

### 9. Policy Updates

If this policy changes materially, we will notify you in the App or in release notes.
